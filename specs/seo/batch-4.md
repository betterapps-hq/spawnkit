This is the final phase. Copy for your agent, blog posts and long-form articles begin after programmatic and agent-infrastructure improvements. Select topics from actual queries, onboarding failures and useful product demonstrations.

## Copy for your agent

- [ ] Add Copy for your agent to the creator journey, kit pages and configured builder.
- [ ] Generate selection-specific instructions from the resolved `@spawn/templates` configuration: destination, engine/modules, prerequisites, verified installation, development and verification commands, conflict handling and optional publishing.

## Editorial tasks

- [ ] Map query intent to one primary destination; consolidate overlapping topics.
- [ ] Publish a flagship guide from idea through AI-assisted iteration, playtesting and publishing, with a working game and downloadable source.
- [ ] Add focused engine, physics, deployment and debugging tutorials based on tested workflows.
- [ ] Publish creator case studies and reproducible research where real evidence exists.
- [ ] Distribute useful example repositories, engine integrations, videos and game-jam outcomes. Obtain creator consent before publishing their work.
- [ ] Review and update successful resources as versions and capabilities change.

Each substantive tutorial includes a real outcome, prerequisites, tested versions, reproducible steps, known limitations, author and useful next action. Avoid arbitrary word counts, fabricated results, keyword variants of the same article and unsupported product claims.

Authority comes from resources people use and reference. Do not buy ranking links, automate community spam or require keyword-rich backlinks in generated projects. An optional creator attribution link should point to a useful relevant destination.
